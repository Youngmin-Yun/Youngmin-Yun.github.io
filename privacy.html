Privacy Policy – WOLF App

Last updated: July 7, 2026

1. Information We Collect

WOLF does not collect or store any personally identifiable information on our servers. The following data may be stored locally on your device:

  • Strava access token (stored securely on device only)
  • App settings (units, goals, preferences)

2. Third-Party Services

WOLF uses the Strava API to retrieve your workout data. This data is used solely within the app and is not transmitted to any external server. Please refer to Strava's Privacy Policy for details.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties.
